What our guides are, and what they aren't.

Last updated 5 August 2026

B-Acoustics publishes guides on our Guides section and service pages covering topics like acoustic ratings, project costs, and the permit or approval processes that apply to renovation and industrial noise projects in Singapore (for example, HDB and MCST approvals, and NEA's boundary noise framework).

General information, not advice

This content is provided as general background for orientation purposes only. It is not legal advice, regulatory advice, financial advice, or a professional acoustic assessment of your specific situation. Nothing on this site creates a client relationship or a substitute for independent professional advice tailored to your circumstances.

Figures, rules, and fees can change

Where a guide references a law, regulation, permit process, timing rule, or penalty, that description reflects our general understanding at the time of writing. Regulators and authorities (including NEA, HDB, and individual MCSTs) update their requirements and figures from time to time, and we do not guarantee that any specific figure, fee, timing rule, or provision described on this site is current. Always confirm current requirements directly with the relevant authority — NEA, HDB, your MCST or building management, the Community Mediation Centre, or a qualified legal professional, as applicable — before relying on this content for a real decision, project, or dispute.

Cost estimates are illustrative

Where a guide discusses what typically drives project cost, those are general explanatory factors, not a quote. Actual pricing depends on your specific site, materials, and requirements — request a site-specific assessment before budgeting a real project.

If you notice something on this site that appears to be outdated or incorrect, we'd genuinely appreciate you letting us know — get in touch.
