Reading the numbers — a quick key
NRC0 — 1
Noise Reduction Coefficient

How much sound a surface soaks up instead of reflecting. Higher means a softer, less echoey room.

STChigher = quieter
Sound Transmission Class

How well a wall or floor blocks sound passing through it. The higher the rating, the quieter the room next door.

dB−10 ≈ ½ as loud
Decibel

The unit of loudness. A drop of about 10 dB sounds roughly half as loud to the human ear.

RT60seconds
Reverberation time

How long a sound lingers before fading away. Shorter times keep speech crisp and intelligible.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does an acoustic consultant actually do?

We measure how sound behaves in your specific space — a baseline noise or reverberation reading — then model, specify and verify a fix, rather than installing generic soundproofing products off a catalogue.

Which property types does B-Acoustics work with?

Commercial fit-outs, residential homes, and industrial sites across Singapore — see Commercial, Residential and Industrial above for what each covers.

What's the difference between STC and NRC ratings?

STC measures how well a wall, floor or door blocks sound between spaces; NRC measures how much sound a material absorbs within a space. See our full STC vs NRC explainer for which one your project needs.

How long does a project take, from enquiry to completion?

It depends on scope, but every project moves through the same five stages — assess, model, specify, build, verify — with a timeline set once the target rating and site access are confirmed.

Do you test and verify results after the work is done?

Yes — verification measurement against the agreed target is the final step of every project, not an optional add-on.
