# Backlink Targets — for you to action

A researched shortlist of real, currently-operating places worth getting
b-acoustics.com listed, to start building the off-page authority flagged as
open in `SEO_RECOMMENDATIONS.md` and `STATUS.md`. **This is research only —
none of these have been submitted to.** Creating directory accounts and
contacting associations both require you directly (account ownership,
business verification, and in some cases professional membership are not
things that can be done on your behalf). Treat this as a checklist, not
a completed action.

## General Singapore business directories (fast, free tier available)

These are broad, low-effort SEO citations — not high-authority individually,
but real, indexed, and free to start with.

- **Yellow Pages Singapore** — [listing.yellowpages.com.sg](https://listing.yellowpages.com.sg/free-plan/) — free SME listing plan; one of the most established SG directories.
- **Opendi Singapore** — local business directory, ~3M monthly visitors per its own listing (verify current traffic claims before treating as fact).
- **Hotfrog Singapore** — free SME listing, general SEO citation value.
- **G Search** — [gsearch.com.sg](https://www.gsearch.com.sg/) — B2B-focused Singapore directory, more relevant than general consumer directories given B-Acoustics' B2B/commercial client base.
- **Yelu.sg** — local business network directory.

Practical note: NAP (name/address/phone) consistency matters more than
volume here — use exactly `228 Changi Road #03-02, Singapore 419741` and
`+65 9641 6999` on every listing, matching the site's own schema (see
`business-legal-entity-2026-07` memory for why the address form matters).

## Acoustic / engineering professional bodies

- **Institute of Acoustics (IOA)** — the body Thomas Ee's MIOA credential
  comes from (displayed on the Studio section already). Worth checking
  whether IOA has a public "member firms" or consultants directory
  separate from individual membership — individual membership doesn't
  automatically create a company listing. Check [the IOA's own site]
  directly for a firm/consultant directory option.

## Architecture & interior-design industry bodies (relevant — most B-Acoustics
work is referred through or alongside architects/designers)

- **Singapore Institute of Architects (SIA)** — [sia.org.sg](https://sia.org.sg/) — publishes a "Capability Profiles" directory of firms serving architects; worth checking if acoustic consultants can be listed as an affiliate/associate capability provider. Requires direct inquiry with SIA — no public self-service listing found.
- **Society of Interior Designers Singapore (SIDS)** — [sid-singapore.org](https://sid-singapore.org/) — professional body for ID firms; check for an affiliate/associate-member category for consultants who work alongside interior designers. Also requires direct inquiry — no public self-service directory found.

## What I did NOT do (and why)

- Did not create accounts on any directory — account creation/ownership
  needs to be yours.
- Did not contact SIA, SIDS, or IOA — outreach on your behalf is outside
  what I can do without your direct involvement each time.
- Did not verify current pricing/traffic claims on every directory beyond
  what's stated on their own marketing pages — treat traffic figures above
  as directory-reported, not independently verified.

## Suggested order

1. Yellow Pages + G Search first (free, fast, real SEO citation value,
   B2B-relevant).
2. SIA and SIDS inquiries next (slower, but higher relevance — your actual
   referral network overlaps with their membership).
3. IOA firm-listing check (worth 10 minutes to see if it exists; skip if
   individual-membership-only).
