Industrial noise compliance in Singapore

Plants, data centres, and workshops operate under statutory noise limits and worker-safety requirements at the same time. We scope both together, rather than treating compliance and worker safety as separate projects.

General information, not compliance advice. Regulatory limits, fees, and procedures can change — always confirm current requirements directly with NEA or a qualified professional. We can help scope and document a site-specific compliance assessment.

Duct attenuation — boundary noise reduction
A

Boundary noise reduction & compliance

Singapore's industrial premises are subject to permissible noise limits at the property boundary, enforced by NEA, which vary by land-use zone and time of day. Before recommending any physical works, we take boundary measurements against the applicable limits so the scope is sized to the actual gap, not a worst-case assumption — and can prepare the documented assessment a compliance submission requires.

B

Plant & duct attenuation

Compressors, generators, chillers, and ductwork are usually the dominant noise source at a site boundary. Duct silencers, acoustic louvres, and enclosure panels sized to the specific equipment are typically more effective — and more cost-efficient — than a general perimeter barrier, because they treat the source rather than the whole site.

C

Vibration & structure-borne noise

Heavy rotating or reciprocating machinery can transmit noise through the building structure itself, showing up as noise in spaces with no direct line of sight to the source. Vibration isolation mounts and, where needed, floating floor construction interrupt this path at the equipment base rather than trying to treat it downstream.

Typical scope: boundary and source noise measurement against applicable NEA limits, an attenuation plan targeted at the specific equipment driving the exceedance, and post-installation verification testing where a compliance report is required.

Frequently asked

What are Singapore's noise limits for industrial premises?

NEA sets permissible noise limits at the property boundary that vary by surrounding land-use zone and time of day — we assess your specific site against the applicable limit rather than working off a generic figure, since the correct threshold depends on location.

Does my factory need an acoustic compliance report?

Many industrial and F&B fit-outs require a documented noise assessment as part of approvals or in response to a complaint — we can scope and deliver this alongside any physical noise-control works.

What's the difference between boundary noise and worker noise exposure?

Boundary noise concerns sound reaching neighbouring premises; worker noise exposure is a separate workplace-safety consideration inside the plant. Both can share the same attenuation works, but they're assessed against different standards.
